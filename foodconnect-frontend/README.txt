FOODCONNECT FRONTEND

Files:
- index.html: homepage structure
- style.css: responsive styling
- script.js: mobile navigation and donation search/category filters
- assets/: your two supplied photos

How to run:
1. Keep the assets folder beside index.html.
2. Open index.html in a browser, or use VS Code Live Server.
3. Replace sample donation cards with data from your Express API when the backend is ready.

Notes:
- The Login, Register and Request Food links currently show a demo toast. Connect them to your real authentication and request pages/API.
- The sample donation listings and statistics are placeholder content.
- The Explore Donations button has a distinct golden background.
